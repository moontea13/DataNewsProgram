After living for a thousand years, Xu Xiao fails a heavenly tribulation, loses his cultivation, and is left with only one year to live. Just as the aging sect elder prepares to die, a delayed ten-thousandfold return system awakens: gifts given to women marked by fortune can return divine weapons, elixirs, and cultivation gains many times over. Each exchange draws him back from collapse, rewarding him with treasures such as the Nanming Li Fire Sword and the Three Thousand Dao Pill until he regains the Nascent Soul realm. But demonic incursions and a gathering of nine sects turn his private recovery into a larger struggle, while those around him still mistake the old man for someone nearing the end of his path.

The story in one line

With one year of life remaining after a failed tribulation, the thousand-year-old Xu Xiao discovers that gifts to women of fortune trigger multiplied returns capable of restoring his cultivation.

Setting and world

The story unfolds across cultivation sects, merchant houses, demonic incursions, and a nine-sect gathering in an eastern-fantasy world where lifespan, realm, and rare treasures determine power.

Narrative framework

Each arc turns a gift into an outsized return, uses the reward to repair Xu Xiao's realm or solve an immediate crisis, and then raises the stakes as more factions notice his renewed strength.

Core story elements

One-year lifespan countdown

The failed tribulation leaves Xu Xiao with a fixed deadline, turning every recovery opportunity into a race against death.

Ten-thousandfold return system

Giving an eligible treasure to a woman of fortune can produce a far more valuable weapon, pill, or cultivation reward.

Treasure-driven realm recovery

Rewards including the Nanming Li Fire Sword and Three Thousand Dao Pill help Xu Xiao rebuild toward the Nascent Soul realm.

Demonic incursion

Nine-sect gathering

A wider meeting of cultivation factions raises the political stakes around the old elder's hidden strength.

Story arcs

Opening phase

Recovery phase
Gifts, multiplied returns, and Nascent Soul

Escalation phase
Demonic pressure and the nine sects

Demonic forces and the nine-sect gathering turn a private survival strategy into a conflict that can affect the wider cultivation world.
